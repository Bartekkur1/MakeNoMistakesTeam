## 1. Problem Statement & Objectives
Previously, MatiBuilds (the scammer NPC) was handled as a single-player encounter. When multiple players were near the NPC:
- Mati had no queueing mechanism for concurrent candidate players within `NOTICE_DISTANCE` (28 studs).
- If multiple players approached, there was risk of conflicting state, re-entrant loops, or abrupt conversation resets.
- When an encounter finished, Mati did not systematically advance to the next awaiting player.

The goal of this quick task was to implement and activate a robust server-side multi-player FIFO queue arbiter adhering to requirement [D-24]:
1. First eligible player to arrive becomes the active owner.
2. Subsequent players arriving within 28 studs are deduplicated and enqueued in FIFO arrival order (tie-break by `UserId`).
3. If an enqueued player walks away (> 32 studs), disconnects, or dies, they are pruned from the queue.
4. When the active encounter ends (via choice, close button, walking away, or timeout), the next player is automatically popped from FIFO and served sequentially.
5. When the queue is drained, Mati resumes idle waypoint patrol with interruptible pauses.

## 2. Key Changes Implemented

### A. Arbiter Enhancement in `roblox/src/server/NPCNavigation.luau`
- Added `isSamePlayer(a, b)` helper comparing by `UserId` (fallback to identity reference) to ensure seamless matching across network serialization boundaries and mock objects.
- Added `isInQueue(player: Player): boolean` and `getQueue(): { Player }`.
- Enhanced `enqueue(player: Player)` with deduplication and sorting by arrival timestamp, with `UserId` tie-break per D-24.
- Added guard to `advanceQueue()` to prevent double-advancing or overwriting an active owner.
- Updated `advanceQueue()` to validate that the popped player has a valid character, root part, and alive humanoid (`Health > 0`).
- Updated `releaseOwner(player: Player)` and `cancelPlayer(player: Player)` to cleanly pop and return the next player in queue.

### B. Proximity Scanner & Worker Orchestration in `roblox/src/server/ScammerNPCManager.server.luau`
- Separated the continuous proximity scanner (`task.wait(0.35)`) from the worker navigation loop.
- Scanner detects approaching eligible players within 28 studs, enqueuing them if not currently owning or in queue.
- Automatically prunes players from queue if distance exceeds `BREAK_DISTANCE` (32 studs).
- Worker loop only pops next player via `arbiter.advanceQueue()` when idle (`not arbiter.getOwner()`).
- Ensured `arbiter.releaseOwner(targetPlayer)` is called unconditionally at the end of every encounter (whether completed, closed, walked away, or timed out).
- Replaced uninterruptible `task.wait(math.random(2, 4))` patrol pause with an interruptible loop that immediately aborts if a player is enqueued or claims ownership.
- Added `QueryArbiter` BindableFunction and `TestEnqueuePlayer` BindableEvent in `MissionRemotes` for introspection and integration testing.

### C. Safety Cleanup in `roblox/src/server/MissionService.server.luau`
- Updated `cancel(player)` to unconditionally invoke `arbiter.releaseOwner(player)` even if the player's conversation state table was already cleaned up or uninitialized.
