- **Found during:** Task 3, PNG review
- **Issue:** `hyphens: auto` has no effect in the export browser. A probe showed Chrome with a fresh profile does not hyphenate pl or en, because the dictionaries are not downloaded. Narrow cards on slides 1, 2, 3, 7, 9 and 10 showed wide gaps.
- **Fix:** Added `&shy;` soft hyphens inside 15 long words in those texts (e.g. Pol&shy;sce, moni&shy;toruje, tele&shy;komu). The wording is unchanged.
- **Commit:** cb72d5b
